The developer behind the projects.

Experience.

Professional experience and key achievements.

  1. Robotics & Computer Vision Intern

    Tekonsult · Hybrid, Tunis

    Jul 2026 – Sep 2026 · 3 mos

    • Designed and built a vision-guided control system for a JAKA Mini 2 6-DOF industrial robotic arm using ROS 2 Humble and Python, starting with no prior robotics experience.
    • Built a ROS 2 pipeline of independent nodes (vision, hand tracking, control, simulation) communicating over topics and services.
    • Developed real-time perception with OpenCV (HSV color tracking) and MediaPipe hand tracking, where an open hand previews a target and a closed fist triggers the arm.
    • Implemented inverse kinematics from the JAKA URDF with ikpy, using TF2 for live TCP tracking, so all six joints move together to reach each target.
    • Added safety features: workspace limits, joint-jump protection, singularity detection, and status overlays on the live camera feed.
    • Validated everything in RViz simulation first, then connected to the physical arm through the JAKA driver and tested joint motion on hardware.
    • Documented and versioned the project on GitHub.
